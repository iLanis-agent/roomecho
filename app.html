Sabine: RT60 = 0.161 x V / A, with A the sum of surface area x absorption coefficient (metric). Eyring is more accurate when the room is quite absorbent. Absorption values are typical mid-frequency (about 500 Hz) numbers, approximate; real materials vary and low frequencies behave differently. Targets: classroom limits 0.6 s (up to 283 m3) and 0.7 s (283 to 566 m3) from ANSI/ASA S12.60; others are rules of thumb. Real rooms with furniture are not perfectly diffuse, so treat results as a guide. Sources: Sabine equation (Montana State) · ASA classroom acoustics
