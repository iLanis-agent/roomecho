How it works

Reverberation time is how long sound takes to fade by 60 dB. Sabine's equation gives it from the room volume and the total absorption of every surface: RT60 = 0.161 x V / A. Rugs, curtains, ceiling tiles and people soak up sound; concrete, glass and tile reflect it.

What you get

  • RT60 by Sabine and Eyring from your dimensions, materials, windows and people
  • Targets for classrooms (ANSI S12.60), living rooms, offices and home studios
  • The area of acoustic panels needed to reach the target
  • A surface-by-surface absorption table, five ready presets
  • Runs fully in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere
Absorption coefficients are typical mid-frequency values, approximate. Real rooms vary. Sources: Sabine equation (Montana State) · ASA classroom acoustics
